# Bryan Chetcuti

Technology leader working across governance, delivery, operational improvement and digital trust.

My work increasingly focuses on a simple question: **how do we make digital trust observable, governable and explainable?**

Current public-interest work includes:

- **[ThreatScope Check](https://threatscopecheck.com/)** — a diagnostic instrument for inspecting the visible trust posture of a domain.
- **[.au Domain Observatory](https://domainobservatory.au/)** — longitudinal observation of visible trust signals across the Australian domain ecosystem.
- **[TrustSurface](https://trustsurface.org/)** — an open framework for understanding and governing digital trust signals.
- **[Domain Governance Baseline](https://baseline.bryanchetcuti.com/)** — practical guidance and self-assessment for domain governance.

I also maintain smaller technical experiments and open artefacts where they are useful.
